## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Full-screen ad services: interstitial, rewarded, rewarded interstitial, app open.
- Banner widget (`AdaptiveBannerAd`) with collapsible support.
- Native ad widget (`NativeAdCard`) with template styling.
- UMP/GDPR consent flow with privacy options.
- Pluggable mediation consent bridge for partner SDKs.
- Analytics event bus with impression-level revenue.
- Exponential backoff retry, TTL expiry, and frequency capping.
- Automatic test ad IDs in debug builds.
